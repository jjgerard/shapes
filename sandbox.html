How the sandbox works

Parts (left) holds every piece as a separate loose piece. Drag one onto the board. Drag it back onto the Parts shelf to put it away.

Joining. Every piece has little cups (the pale circles). Drag a piece so its top cup is near a bottom cup of another piece (or the other way round) and let go: they join with a stick, which comes in three lengths (20, 40 and 60 mm), so the piece settles at the nearest of those distances. Nothing stops you joining any piece to any other; what goes with what is yours to work out.

Moving. Drag a piece to move it together with everything hanging from it. Dragging a piece that hangs from another pulls it off.

Words are loose magnets. Drop one under a head (or under an abbreviation triangle) and it stays with it. Double-click a word to cross it out, for a trace.

Workbench → Box → Table. Build a piece of structure on the Workbench, select it and press Put in the box (or drag it onto the Box). It is saved there with a name you can change. Switch to the Table and drag copies out of the Box to assemble whole trees. Pieces from the shelf work on either board.

Everything is saved in this browser. Save picture downloads the board as an image.
