How the sandbox works

Parts holds every piece loose. Tap a piece to put it on the table, or drag it there (on a phone, drag it upward). Drag a piece back onto the Parts to put it away.

XP or X′ mode (top) decides which pieces you get and what may join what, the same rules as the game: flat phrase structure in XP mode, the bar level in X′ mode.

Joining. Every piece has little cups (the pale circles). Drag a piece so its top cup is near a bottom cup of another and let go: they join with a stick of 20, 40 or 60 mm. A wrong join makes both pieces glow red. Drag a piece to move it with everything hanging from it; dragging a piece that hangs from another pulls it off.

Words are loose magnets: drop one under a head (or an abbreviation triangle). Double-tap a word, or select it and press “Cross out”, for a trace.

Tinker is free play. Build a sentence suggests a sentence (a basic statement, a question or a passive) and can check that your tree is built correctly.

Move around with one finger (or the mouse) on the empty table; pinch or use the wheel to zoom. Everything is saved in this browser.
