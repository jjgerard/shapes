# Block kit: circle-and-spokes connectors, derived from the game

Generated by `physical/spokes.py` from `data.js` (via `physical/dump_rules.js`). Do not edit by hand.

One kit, two ways to use it. The XP pieces are shared; only the heads come in two kinds. **XP-heads fit only XP pieces, X′-heads fit only X′ pieces**, so a head cannot skip the bar level in X′ mode, and a tree with all its words placed is one mode or the other.

- 12 spoke positions evenly spaced every 30.0° on a 18.7 mm core: one thick orientation spoke plus 6 deep (A) and 5 shallow (B) code positions = 30 codes, 26 used.
- Spokes 2.4 mm wide (orientation spoke 4.2 mm), 2.0 mm tall, clearance 0.15 mm per side.
- **Wrong-pair test:** 676 plug x socket pairs checked geometrically: wrongly fitting **0**, correct pairs that fail **0**.
- **Orientation:** every plug at every 6° of rotation against every socket seats at a wrong rotation **0** times (without the thick spoke: 144).
- **Self-check:** the geometry test flagged wrong fits in 20 of 20 deliberately invalid code assignments.

## The game's own trees

Every Level 1 target and Level 2 sentence tree was checked to be physically buildable from the kit (every node's children fit its slots, one per slot, in order):

- **XP mode (flat):** 7 trees, 80 nodes, **0** that cannot be built.
- **X′ mode (X-bar):** 7 trees, 153 nodes, **0** that cannot be built.
