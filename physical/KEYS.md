# Connector design, from the game's own shapes

Generated by `physical/keys.py` from `data.js` (via `physical/dump_rules.js`). Do not edit by hand.

A slot takes the shape it shows: the plug on an arm is the outline of the child it takes, the socket on top of a piece is the outline of that piece. Same outline at a different level is told apart by a pin pattern.

- Every silhouette is scaled to the area of a 20 mm circle, so no shape fits inside a different one at any rotation (checked: 0 cases).
- Corners rounded to 0.9 mm radius; clearance 0.15 mm per side (tune with the coupon).
