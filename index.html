Choose a level

Level 1 — Shapes

Each sub-level gives you a fixed set of pieces — use every one of them, connected into a single shape, with no shortcuts. Finish one to unlock the next.

Level 2 — Words

Every piece is already labeled — drag each word from the bank onto the piece it belongs to. Finish one sentence to unlock the next.

Level 3 — Constituents

A string of words from the sentence is highlighted below the tree — is it a constituent (the whole yield of some single piece) or not? Get a run of correct answers to finish.

Level 4 — Categories

A constituent from the sentence is highlighted below the tree — click the matching category sticker. Get a run of correct answers to finish.

Mystery Level

Here's a shape you built. Type what you think each shape and each number stands for — a short word or abbreviation is fine.
