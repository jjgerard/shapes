Level 1 — Shapes

Every piece is already labeled — drag each word from the bank onto the piece it belongs to. Finish one sentence to unlock the next.

Level 4 — Categories

A constituent from the sentence is highlighted below the tree — click the matching category sticker. Get 10 in a row to finish.
